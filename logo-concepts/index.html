IDENTITY EXPLORATION · AUGUST 2026

R.K. Bansal Finance
Five marks, one house.

Navy #112f5b · minted gold #b88624/#d9a53f · warm ivory #fbf8f2. Every concept keeps the house double-stroke, the minted-gold metal, and the rupee at the center of the story — they differ in how loudly they speak: from struck-coin heritage to Swiss-modern restraint.

01Heritage, evolved

The Minted Monogram

A direct evolution of the current coin — but struck, not drawn. Machined bezel ticks, the house double-ring, and an engraved Garamond RKB monogram under a small ₹ crest. Reads as a mint mark on a sovereign: four decades of lending, pressed into metal.

02Trust & security

The Keystone

A peaked shield — part vault door, part keystone — carrying the rupee. The navy plate with an inset gold hairline is the site’s double-stroke motif made monumental. The most institutional of the five: RBI-registered, fully secured, no hidden charges, in one shape.

03Modern & editorial

The Bleed Mark

An oversized minted-gold ₹ cropped by the tile — money bigger than the frame. The crop is the modern move; the gold gradient and inner hairline keep it in the house. Strongest as an app icon and social avatar; instantly ownable at any size.

04Swiss & confident

The Ledger Wordmark

No emblem — the name is the mark. Heavy Jakarta grotesque “RKB” closed by a single minted bead: the full stop is a coin. Underneath, the registrar’s line in tracked caps. The most contemporary option; the bead alone becomes the favicon.

05Banknote cinema

The Guilloché Seal

The hero seal’s security-engraving language distilled into a static mark: a twenty-lobe guilloché braid between coin rings, rupee at the core. It is literally the site’s motion identity at rest — the strongest brand-system alignment of the five.

All marks are vector (SVG), drawn on a 120-grid, and hold at 18 px. Type set in Plus Jakarta Sans (grotesque) and EB Garamond (engraved serif) per the site’s system. Final artwork would outline all glyphs and ship mono / reversed / clear-space specs.
